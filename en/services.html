Main activity

Generation and sale of electricity

SOLARMA generates solar electricity through its photovoltaic plant in Peveragno and feeds it into the grid. The activity includes managing the contracts relating to the sale of the energy and the obligations towards the distribution operator and the national energy services agency.

The company’s articles also provide for trading in electricity and heat produced from renewable sources — from company-owned plants, plants under its management or third-party plants — and for dealing in rights, contracts and certificates representing electricity.

  • Generation from a company-owned photovoltaic plant
  • Grid feed-in and sale of the electricity produced
  • Management of obligations towards the grid operator and the energy agency
  • Trading in renewable energy
  • Dealing in instruments and certificates representing electricity
Engineering

Design, works supervision and testing

The company’s objects include architectural and structural design — preliminary, detailed and executive — as well as works supervision and testing in civil and industrial construction, plant engineering, agriculture and environmental protection.

Our approach is that of an owner-operator: realistic sizing, attention to operating costs and verification of yield assumptions before they become contractual commitments.

  • Feasibility studies and yield analysis
  • Preliminary, detailed and executive design
  • Architectural and structural design
  • Works supervision and testing
  • Grid connection and permitting procedures
Construction

Renewable energy installations

The company builds and installs plants and equipment for the production of energy from renewable sources, using technologies available on the market and in particular photovoltaic systems producing electricity and heat.

This work can be carried out on our own account or for third parties, whether public or private, in Italy and abroad.

  • Photovoltaic plants for electricity generation
  • Solar systems for heat production
  • Installation of plant equipment and components
  • Coordination of contractors on site
  • Functional testing and commissioning
Operation

Running, management and maintenance

Maintenance, operation and management of plants are part of our daily work on our own installation and are offered to third parties as well. A plant that is followed continuously produces more and degrades more slowly than one left to itself.

The service can be structured as a continuing contract or as a one-off inspection, depending on the size of the plant and the owner’s needs.

  • Output monitoring and deviation analysis
  • Planned routine maintenance
  • Fault diagnosis and repair
  • Periodic checks of electrical protection devices
  • Support with obligations towards public bodies

Scope of activity. All the activities listed are carried out within the limits of, and in compliance with, the rules governing them, both on our own account and on behalf of third parties, whether public or private. Activities reserved to regulated professions are expressly excluded from the company’s objects.
