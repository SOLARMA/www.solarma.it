The plant

Peveragno, Cuneo — in operation since 2012

Our production unit is a photovoltaic power plant in via Boves 269, Peveragno, in the province of Cuneo. It entered service on 28 June 2012 and has been the core of the company’s activity ever since.

  • Company-owned plant, connected to the distribution grid
  • Output monitoring and planned maintenance
  • More than a decade of continuous operation

Sustainability

Every kilowatt-hour generated is a kilowatt-hour not burnt

Solar energy is a local, predictable resource with no emissions during operation. Generating it close to where it is consumed reduces grid losses and dependence on imported fossil fuels.

  • Distributed generation in the province of Cuneo
  • No direct CO₂ emissions during operation
  • A plant integrated into its surroundings, with no water consumption
  • End-of-life components handled through dedicated recycling channels
